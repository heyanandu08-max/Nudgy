# Delete your account

You can delete your Nudgy account and data at any time. It can't be undone.

## In the app

1. Open Nudgy → Settings → Privacy.
2. Choose **Delete my account**, then confirm.

This deletes your account, usage records, shared links and (if you own a team) the team and
its courses, and cancels any PayPal subscription. **Delete learning history** in the same
place wipes the lessons, skills, questions and walkthroughs stored on that computer.

## Without the app

Email [{{email}}](mailto:{{email}}) from the address you signed in with, with the subject
"Delete my account". We delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

## What is deleted, and what is kept

| Data | After deletion |
| --- | --- |
| Account, email, consent record | Deleted |
| Usage records, team runs, shared links and courses | Deleted |
| Subscription | Cancelled in PayPal |
| Payment records held by PayPal | Kept by PayPal as the law requires |
| Data on your own computer | Stays until you use "Delete learning history" or uninstall |
