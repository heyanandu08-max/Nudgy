# Cookie Policy

Last updated: {{updated}}

**Short version:** the Nudgy app uses no cookies, and our web pages use none unless you accept
optional analytics.

## The app

The desktop app keeps your settings and learning history in its own data folder on your
computer. It sets no cookies and contains no advertising or analytics trackers.

## Our web pages

The pages on {{site}} (sign-in, shared walkthroughs, these legal pages):

| What | Type | Purpose | Kept for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `nudgy-consent` | Browser storage (not sent to us) | Remembers your answer to the cookie question | Until you clear it |
| Analytics cookies | Optional, only if you click Accept | Counts visits so we know which pages help | Set by the analytics provider |

When analytics is turned on for the site, a banner asks first. "Decline" is as easy as
"Accept", nothing optional loads before you choose, and you can change your mind by clearing
this site's data in your browser.
