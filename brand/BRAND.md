# Nudgy brand

## Idea
The logo is "nudgy" with the **y knocked over**, as if the g just nudged it. Two small tick marks show the bump. The joke stays inside the logo, so nothing else in the product needs to try hard.

- **JetBrains Mono** 400: dates, meta lines, keycaps, app badges

## The Nudgy cursor
The desktop companion is a **second cursor**: a black arrow with a white outline, tilted 16° like the knocked-over "y" in the logo. It trails your real cursor, flies to the button you need, and shows what it's doing with a tiny label ("listening", "thinking ···", "watching", "nice ✓").
It always works on top of the app you're using, never inside Nudgy's own window.
No emoji anywhere in the product.

## Voice
Plain, short, friendly, like a friend next to you. Lowercase is fine for small labels.
- ✅ "Select your numbers, then hit Σ Sum up here."
- ✅ "your turn · I'm watching this window only"
- ❌ "Unlock the power of AI to supercharge your workflow!"
Never say "AI-powered", "seamless", "effortless", "Certainly!", "Great question!", or "As an AI".
